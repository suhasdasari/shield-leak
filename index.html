Shield Leak

Fhenix confidential tokens encrypt balances and transfer amounts. Shield amounts, payout amounts, counterparties and timing stay public. Paste an address or transaction to see what anyone can read from it.

Token board

Every contract found emitting ConfidentialTransfer whose name() starts with "Fhenix". Amounts are in token units (6-decimal confidential precision). "Crowd" is the number of distinct addresses that ever shielded on that token, which is the most anyone can blend into.

Link finder

Payouts (public unshield events) whose exact amount matches a shield made earlier on the same token. If only one address ever shielded that amount, the payout is flagged as a unique-amount match. Same-address round trips are shown separately. These are heuristics, not proof of ownership.

Before you unshield

Amounts already seen on each token. Exiting with an amount several others have used, and not right after your own shield, makes a payout harder to tie to one deposit. With crowds this small, no amount blends in well yet.

What is public, per Fhenix docs

  • Shield: the amount is in call data and in the underlying token's Transfer into the wrapper.
  • Unshield + payout: the burned amount is made publicly decryptable and the payout event carries the decrypted amount.
  • Confidential transfer: sender, receiver and time are visible; the amount is encrypted.

Sources: Shield and unshield, best practices, FHERC20 wrapper.
